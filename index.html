About Tom Conlon

Download approved media assets for interviews, features, and event listings.

Tom Conlon is a former journalist and longtime agency founder now building an AI product inside the industry AI is upending. He's spent his career watching how information moves, how agencies adapt, and how brands lose the plot when the rules change. Moonvine grew out of that problem: a brand insights platform that pulls together AI visibility and broader market signals, then turns them into a weekly read on what changed, why it matters, and what to do next.
